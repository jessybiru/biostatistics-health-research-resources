
# Regression & Statistical Modelling Resources

A curated collection of freely accessible learning resources covering regression analysis, statistical modelling, model interpretation and practical applications in biostatistics, epidemiology and health research.

These resources include online course materials, worked examples, datasets and statistical programming exercises.
### 1. Regression Methods (STAT 501)

**Provider:** Pennsylvania State University  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the free course notes](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat501/)

An applied course covering the principles, assumptions, estimation and interpretation of regression models.

**Includes:**
- Simple and multiple linear regression
- Categorical predictors and interaction effects
- Regression diagnostics and influential observations
- Multicollinearity and data transformations
- Model building and prediction
- Worked examples and learning materials

**Best for:** Postgraduate students, researchers and lecturers teaching regression analysis.

### 2. Analysis of Discrete Data (STAT 504)

**Provider:** Pennsylvania State University  
**Level:** Intermediate to advanced  
**Resource:** [Access the free course notes](https://online.stat.psu.edu/stat504/)

A graduate-level course on categorical data analysis and generalised linear models, with examples using R and SAS.

**Includes:**
- Contingency tables and measures of association
- Logistic regression
- Poisson and log-linear models
- Multinomial and ordinal logistic regression
- Model diagnostics and goodness-of-fit assessment
- Interpretation of main effects and interactions

**Best for:** Biostatisticians and epidemiologists analysing binary, categorical and count outcomes.

### 3. UCLA Data Analysis Examples

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Beginner to advanced  
**Resource:** [Explore data analysis examples](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/other/dae/)

A practical collection of statistical analysis examples organised by method and software.

**Includes:**
- Linear and logistic regression
- Poisson and negative binomial regression
- Ordinal and multinomial logistic regression
- Robust regression
- Mixed-effects models
- Example commands and datasets for multiple statistical packages

**Best for:** Researchers looking for practical examples of statistical models in R, Stata or SAS.

### 4. UCLA Annotated Statistical Output

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Explore annotated output](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/other/annotatedoutput/)

A collection of statistical output examples with explanations of how to interpret the results.

**Includes:**
- Regression coefficients and standard errors
- Odds ratios and statistical significance
- Linear, logistic and count regression
- Ordinal and multinomial models
- Software-specific examples

**Best for:** Students and researchers who want to understand and report statistical results correctly.
