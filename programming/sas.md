# SAS Programming & Statistical Analysis Resources

A curated collection of freely accessible SAS learning resources, including official training courses, tutorials, statistical examples, datasets and practical exercises for biostatistics, epidemiology and health research.

### 1. SAS OnDemand for Academics

**Provider:** SAS Institute  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Resource:** [Access SAS OnDemand for Academics](https://www.sas.com/en_us/software/on-demand-for-academics.html)

A free, cloud-based SAS learning environment that allows students, educators and independent learners to practise SAS programming and statistical analysis without installing SAS locally.

**Includes:** SAS Studio, introductory tutorials and access to the free SAS Programming 1 and Statistics 1 courses.

**Best for:** Beginners who want to learn SAS but do not have access to licensed SAS software.

**Note:** Free access is intended for learning and teaching purposes.

### 2. SAS Programming for R Users

**Provider:** SAS Institute / SAS Communities  
**Level:** Intermediate to advanced  
**Course materials:** [GitHub repository](https://github.com/sascommunities/sas-prog-for-r-users)  
**Online course:** [SAS Programming for R Users](https://learn.sas.com/course/view.php?id=295)

A comprehensive course designed for experienced R users who want to apply their existing programming and statistical analysis skills in SAS.

**Includes:**
- More than 600 pages of course notes
- Over 80 SAS programs
- Sample datasets in CSV, Excel, text and SAS formats
- Course demonstrations and exercises
- Examples of regression, logistic regression, mixed models, simulation and data manipulation

**Best for:** Researchers, statisticians and data analysts transitioning from R to SAS.

**Note:** Some older setup instructions refer to SAS University Edition, which has been discontinued. Learners can explore SAS OnDemand for Academics instead.  Also Workshop examples were developed using SAS 9.4 and SAS/STAT 15.2. Users should check compatibility with their installed versions.

### 3. Introduction to Survival Analysis in SAS

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the survival analysis seminar](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/sas/seminars/sas-survival/)

A practical introduction to survival analysis using data from 500 participants in the Worcester Heart Attack Study.

**Includes:**
- Downloadable study dataset
- Kaplan–Meier survival estimation
- PROC LIFETEST
- Cox proportional hazards regression using PROC PHREG
- Model diagnostics and proportional hazards assessment
- Time-varying covariates

**Best for:** Clinical researchers, epidemiologists and postgraduate students analysing time-to-event outcomes.

### 4. Logistic Regression in SAS

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the logistic regression seminar](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/sas/seminars/logistic-regression-in-sas/)

A practical seminar demonstrating how to fit, interpret and assess logistic regression models using SAS.

**Includes:** SAS code, example data, model interpretation, goodness-of-fit assessment, nonlinear relationships and interaction effects.

**Best for:** Health researchers and postgraduate students analysing binary outcomes.

### 5. Applied Survey Data Analysis Using SAS

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate to advanced  
**Resource:** [Access the survey analysis workshop](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/sas/seminars/sas-survey/)

A practical workshop on analysing data collected using complex survey sampling designs.

**Includes:**
- Downloadable presentation slides
- SAS code and example datasets
- Survey weights, stratification and clustering
- PROC SURVEYMEANS and PROC SURVEYFREQ
- PROC SURVEYREG and PROC SURVEYLOGISTIC
- Survey-weighted regression examples

**Best for:** Researchers analysing population-based health surveys, including NHANES and other complex survey datasets.

### 6. UCLA SAS Statistical Workshops

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Beginner to advanced  
**Resource:** [Browse the statistical seminar collection](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/other/mult-pkg/seminars/)

A collection of applied SAS workshops covering statistical programming, data analysis and modelling.

**Topics include:**
- Introduction to SAS
- Regression and logistic regression
- Repeated measures analysis
- Multiple imputation
- Survival analysis
- Survey data analysis
- Interactions and SAS macros

**Best for:** Researchers and lecturers looking for method-specific SAS tutorials and teaching examples.

### 7. SAS Annotated Statistical Output

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Explore SAS output examples](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/sas/output/)

Worked statistical examples designed to help learners understand the results generated by SAS procedures.

**Includes:** Statistical output and explanations of coefficients, test statistics, significance tests and model results.

**Best for:** Students and researchers who want to improve their interpretation of statistical analyses.
