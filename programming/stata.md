
# Stata Programming & Statistical Analysis Resources

A curated collection of freely accessible Stata tutorials, statistical workshops, worked examples, datasets and practical learning materials for biostatistics, epidemiology and health research.

### 1. Stata Learning Modules

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the learning modules](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/stata/modules/)

A collection of tutorials covering Stata fundamentals, data management, statistical tests, graphics and working with datasets.

**Includes:** Command examples, explanations and demonstrations of common Stata workflows.

**Best for:** Students and researchers learning Stata or strengthening their data management skills.

### 2. Stata Annotated Output

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Beginner to advanced  
**Resource:** [Explore annotated output](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/stata/output/stata-annotated-output/)

A collection of worked statistical examples explaining how to interpret Stata output.

**Includes:** Examples covering linear and logistic regression, Poisson and negative binomial regression, ordinal and multinomial models, factor analysis and other statistical procedures.

**Best for:** Researchers who want to understand and correctly interpret statistical results.

### 3. Survival Analysis with Stata

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the survival analysis seminar](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/stata/seminars/stata-survival/)

An applied introduction to time-to-event analysis using data from a substance-use treatment study.

**Includes:** Kaplan–Meier curves, log-rank tests, Cox proportional hazards regression, assessment of proportional hazards, model diagnostics and Stata commands.

**Best for:** Clinical researchers, epidemiologists and postgraduate students analysing time-to-event outcomes.

### 4. Survey Data Analysis in Stata

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the survey analysis seminar](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/stata/seminars/survey-data-analysis-with-stata-15/)

A practical guide to analysing complex survey data using Stata, with examples based on the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES).

**Includes:** Survey weights, stratification, clustering, survey design specification, descriptive analysis and regression examples.

**Best for:** Researchers working with DHS, NHANES and other complex survey datasets.

### 5. Multiple Imputation in Stata

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate to advanced  
**Resource:** [Access the multiple imputation workshop](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/stata/seminars/multiple-imputation-in-stata-new/)

A workshop on handling missing data using multiple imputation in Stata.

**Includes:** Workshop slides, downloadable Stata code and explanations of missing-data mechanisms and multiple imputation procedures.

**Best for:** Postgraduate students and researchers dealing with incomplete datasets.

### 6. Stata Textbook Examples

**Provider:** UCLA Office of Advanced Research Computing  
**Level:** Intermediate to advanced  
**Resource:** [Explore textbook examples](https://stats.oarc.ucla.edu/stata/examples/)

A collection of Stata examples illustrating statistical methods from published textbooks.

**Includes:** Replicable analyses and downloadable datasets covering regression, longitudinal analysis, multilevel modelling, survival analysis and other statistical methods.

**Best for:** Students, lecturers and researchers who want to reproduce textbook analyses using Stata.

**Note:** The examples and datasets are accessible online; the associated textbooks are not necessarily free.

### 7. Stata Basics

**Provider:** StataCorp  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Resource:** [Access Stata Basics](https://www.stata.com/links/stata-basics/)

Official introductory learning materials demonstrating how to perform common tasks in Stata.

**Includes:** Worked examples, command syntax, explanations, links to video demonstrations and relevant Stata manuals.

**Best for:** Beginners learning Stata and experienced users reviewing specific commands.
