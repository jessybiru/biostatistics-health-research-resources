# 📚 Biostatistics & Health Research Resources

### Free learning resources for biostatistics, epidemiology, statistical programming and health research

Welcome to this repository!!

This is a curated collection of freely accessible textbooks, online courses, statistical tutorials, practical exercises, datasets, code examples and teaching materials.

As a biostatistician and researcher, I regularly come across valuable learning resources that can help students, researchers and professionals strengthen their statistical and analytical skills.

I have been sharing many of these resources on LinkedIn and created this repository to bring them together in one place, making them easier to find and revisit.

---

##  Who is this repository for?

- Postgraduate students studying biostatistics, epidemiology and public health
- Researchers working with health and population data
- Biostatisticians, statisticians and data analysts
- Lecturers looking for practical teaching materials
- Beginners learning statistical programming
- Professionals interested in applied statistical methods

## 📘 R Programming

### 1. R for Data Science (2nd Edition)

**Authors:** Hadley Wickham, Mine Çetinkaya-Rundel and Garrett Grolemund  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Read the free online book](https://r4ds.hadley.nz/)

A practical introduction to working with data in R, covering data import, cleaning, transformation, visualisation and reproducible reporting.

**Includes:** R code, worked examples, exercises and a companion exercise-solutions website.

### 2. R for Health Data Science

**Authors:** Ewen Harrison and Riinu Pius  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Read the free online book](https://argoshare.is.ed.ac.uk/healthyr_book/)

An applied introduction to R for healthcare professionals and health researchers. Covers data manipulation, visualisation, regression, survival analysis, missing data and reproducible reporting.

**Includes:** Health-related examples, R code and practical demonstrations.

### 3. The Epidemiologist R Handbook

**Authors:** Neale Batra and contributing epidemiologists  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the handbook](https://www.epirhandbook.com/en/)

A task-oriented R reference for epidemiology and public health, with examples covering common epidemiological data management, analysis and reporting tasks.

**Includes:** Example datasets, reproducible R code and an offline-access option.

### 4. Introduction to R for Health Data Analysis

**Authors:** Ehsan Karim, An Hoang and Yang Qu  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Resource:** [Read the online textbook](https://ehsanx.github.io/intro2R/)

An introduction to R programming and data wrangling using the National Health and Nutrition Examination Survey (NHANES).

**Includes:** Health survey examples, R code and practical learning activities.


### 5. Statistical Practice in Epidemiology using R (SPE-R)

**Provider:** SPE-R course team  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the course materials](https://spe-r.github.io/)

A practical course in statistical methods for epidemiological research using R.

**Includes:** Lecture slides, downloadable datasets, R scripts, a practical exercises booklet and a separate booklet with worked solutions.

**Best for:** Epidemiology students, postgraduate researchers and lecturers looking for ready-to-use practical teaching materials.

### 6. Mastering Health Data Science Using R

**Author:** Alice Paul  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Read the online book](https://alicepaul.github.io/health-data-science-using-r/)

An applied R textbook using public health examples to teach data management, visualisation, hypothesis testing, regression and programming.

**Includes:** Case studies involving tuberculosis screening, COVID-19, blood lead levels and hypertension, tuberculosis risk prediction and simulation studies. Also provides exercises, Quarto materials and downloadable source files.

**Best for:** Public health students and health researchers interested in applying R to real research questions.

### 7. Modern Statistics with R (2nd Edition)

**Author:** Måns Thulin  
**Level:** Beginner to advanced  
**Resource:** [Read the free online book](https://modernstatisticswithr.com/)

A comprehensive introduction to modern statistical analysis using R, from data wrangling and exploratory analysis to statistical inference, predictive modelling and simulation.

**Includes:** More than 200 exercises with worked solutions, example datasets and R code.

**Best for:** Students, researchers and lecturers seeking a structured statistics textbook with practical exercises.

### 8. Applied Epi: Free R Tutorials

**Provider:** Applied Epi  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Resource:** [Access the tutorials](https://appliedepi.org/resources/tutorials.html)

Self-paced R tutorials developed around realistic public health data challenges.

**Includes:** Interactive exercises covering R fundamentals, importing data, cleaning datasets and other practical epidemiological tasks. Available in multiple languages.

**Access:** Free account registration is required.

**Best for:** Beginners learning R for epidemiology, surveillance and public health practice.

### 9. Applied Epi: Epidemiology Case Studies

**Provider:** Applied Epi and collaborating organisations  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Explore the case study repository](https://casestudies.appliedepi.org/)

A collection of practical case studies designed to help learners apply R to epidemiological investigations and public health data analysis.

**Includes:** Realistic scenarios, tasks, questions and generated or anonymised datasets. Topics include outbreak investigations and surveillance data analysis.

**Best for:** Epidemiologists, public health practitioners and researchers who want additional hands-on experience.

### 10. swirl: Learn R, in R

**Provider:** swirl project  
**Level:** Beginner  
**Resource:** [Visit swirl](https://swirlstats.com/)

An interactive learning package that teaches R programming directly inside the R console.

**Includes:** Guided lessons and exercises with immediate feedback.

**Getting started:**

```r
install.packages("swirl")
library(swirl)
swirl()
```

**Best for:** Beginners who prefer learning R by running commands rather than only reading tutorials.

### 11. An Introduction to Statistical Learning with Applications in R (2nd Edition)

**Authors:** Gareth James, Daniela Witten, Trevor Hastie and Robert Tibshirani  
**Level:** Intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the free textbook](https://www.statlearning.com/)

A widely used introduction to statistical learning, covering regression, classification, resampling, regularisation, tree-based methods, survival analysis and unsupervised learning.

**Includes:** Free textbook PDF, R practical labs and companion video lectures.

**Best for:** Researchers and students interested in statistical learning and predictive modelling.

### 12. Practical Statistics in Medicine with R

**Author:** Konstantinos I. Bougioukas  
**Level:** Beginner to intermediate  
**Resource:** [Access the online survival analysis chapter](https://practical-stats-med-r.netlify.app/survival)

A practical medical statistics resource demonstrating statistical concepts and methods using R and healthcare examples.

**Includes:** R code and worked explanations of censoring, survival probabilities, Kaplan–Meier estimation, median survival and follow-up, log-rank tests, hazard ratios and Cox proportional hazards regression.

**Best for:** Medical researchers and postgraduate students learning survival analysis.

**Note:** The linked online chapter is accessible for reading; the commercially published textbook is not necessarily available as a free PDF.
